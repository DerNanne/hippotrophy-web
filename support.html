HippoTrophy Support

HippoTrophy for iPhone · Updated November 12, 2026

HippoTrophy is a workout log for strength training. Questions, bug reports and ideas are all welcome.

Contact: hippotrophy@gmail.com

What helps in a message

  • What you did and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → HippoTrophy
  • For a problem with a backup: the backup file itself

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server; nothing you log leaves the device unless you export it yourself. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I back up my data or move to a new iPhone?

In the app, tap Backup on the main screen, then Export Backup, and save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install HippoTrophy, tap Backup → Import Backup and pick that file. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data as well.

A backup cannot be read

Backups from early test versions of the app are in a format it no longer reads. Send the file to the address above and you will get it back converted. For any other file the app cannot open, send it too.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Since all data lives in the app on your device, this removes it completely. Single workouts and exercises can be deleted in the app itself.

More questions

Settings → FAQ in the app explains the gestures, the small icons and the one-sided modes. Settings → What's New lists what each update brought.
